## The Purpose of Life
Every human wonders: Why am I here? Who created this universe? What happens after death?

Islam answers these questions clearly: You were created by Allah to worship Him and live a meaningful life.

## Attributes of the Creator
Allah is the Almighty Creator, perfect in His essence and attributes, self-sufficient and worthy of worship alone.

## The Purpose of Creation
Allah created us with a purpose — to worship Him — and we are tested in this life before resurrection and judgment.

## The Right of God
To worship Him alone and associate no partners with Him. Worship is only for the One who deserves it: the Creator, Sustainer, and Controller.

## Why Islam?
Islam is the universal message sent through all prophets, from Adam to Muhammad ﷺ, calling to the worship of the One True God.

## The Qur’an
The Qur’an is Allah’s final, preserved, miraculous book revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, containing logical, scientific, and spiritual proofs.

## Life After Death
Just as Allah brings plants from dead land, He will resurrect humans for the Day of Judgment and final justice.

## A Call to Reflect
Is it rational to worship idols or created beings? Only the One who created all is worthy of worship.

## Conclusion
Islam is the religion of reason and fitrah (natural disposition), bringing peace in this life and the hereafter. Say now: I testify that there is no god but Allah and Muhammad is His Messenger.
