##The Existence of the Creator
Man often wonders in his rational journey about the origin and creation of the universe:
Who created the heavens and the earth and all that is within them?
How does this vast universe continue to exist in such a perfectly ordered manner through laws that have governed it precisely for centuries?

Did this universe create itself?
Did it come from nothing?
Or did it simply appear by coincidence?

Who created you, O human?

If you were to look at your body under a microscope, you would witness a world of precision and wonder. Every single cell in your body carries genetic information encoded in complex sequences. Have you ever asked yourself: Who wrote this code?
Who placed it precisely where it needs to be?

Every tall structure, every simple house—each tells the story of its builder. Can we then imagine that this vast and intricate universe, with all its beauty and precision, came into existence without a Creator?

Just as your mind would never accept that a sophisticated phone appeared out of nowhere without a designer or creator—or that a complex network of devices works in harmony without a programmer or supervisor—then a sound mind cannot accept that this magnificent universe, with its intricate system, appeared without a Creator.

The Quran guides the mind to reflect deeply on this essential question. Allah the Creator, the Most High, says:

﴿أَمْ خُلِقُوا مِنْ غَيْرِ شَيْءٍ أَمْ هُمُ الْخَالِقُونَ﴾ [الطور: 35]

The meaning:
Were they created out of nothing? That is impossible—non-existence cannot create anything.
Did they create themselves? That too is impossible—nothing can create itself.
Thus, the only logical conclusion remains: This universe must have a Great and Capable Creator.

Every single piece of this universe, from the smallest atom to the largest galaxy, is a sign of the greatness of its Creator. It is logically impossible that such an organized and precise system emerged from chaos.
The intellect rejects the idea that this magnificent order came from nothing or is the result of blind chance.
## The Attributes of the Creator

Just as sound intellect and pure nature lead a person to believe in the existence of a Creator for the universe, they also urge us to reflect on the attributes of this Creator and His rights upon us.

Contemplating the Creator’s attributes opens the doors to deep understanding that He must be characterized by absolute perfection and be free from all deficiencies.

This means that the Creator possesses all the attributes of beauty, majesty, perfection, and greatness. He is the One who is capable of all things, all-knowing of everything, and wise in all that He creates and decrees.

This perfection is not just a possibility—it is a logical necessity. Any deficiency in His attributes would result in imperfection in His creation and planning, which contradicts the evident precision and excellence we observe in the universe.

Moreover, His being free from imperfection implies that the Creator is independent of His creation. He is in no need of anyone; rather, all creation is in need of Him for their existence and survival.

He is exalted above having any partner or offspring, for those are signs of deficiency and weakness—and Allah, the Almighty, is far above such things.

Looking at the universe with reflection and thought leads us to certainty that the One who brought it into existence with such precision and beauty must be a Creator who is perfect in His essence, attributes, and actions—free from any flaw or shortcoming—and deserving of exclusive worship and submission.

Affirming the attributes of perfection for the Lord implies His oneness, and that He alone is uniquely perfect above all else.

Absolute perfection befits only one. Therefore, He alone deserves to be worshiped, with no partner.

And Allah is not just the initiator of the universe; He is its owner and controller. Everything is under His command, and nothing happens in this universe except by His permission and will.

He is the Provider, who guarantees sustenance for His servants, and has created for them the means of life and survival.

So if Allah is the Great Creator, characterized by attributes of perfection and greatness—He who brought us and the universe into existence from nothing, owns everything in it, provides for us, surrounds us with His mercy, and controls everything—

Then shouldn’t we thank Him, seek His pleasure, fulfill our duty toward Him, and work to achieve the purpose for which He created us?
## The Purpose of Creation

Is it logical that Allah would create us without making clear what He wants from us?

Why are we here? What is the purpose and objective of our existence in life?

What will happen to us after death?

Reflect with me: if someone built a grand palace and equipped it with the latest technology, but then suddenly demolished it completely after finishing its construction—would such a person be considered wise? Isn't such behavior obviously aimless and unbefitting of a rational mind?

So if such an action is unacceptable from a human, how could we imagine that Allah—the All-Wise, the All-Knowing—created this wondrous and perfect universe only to destroy it without purpose or wisdom?

Allah created humans for a great and noble purpose: to worship Him alone with no partner, and to test them in this worldly life. He appointed a Day of Resurrection on which they will be raised and held accountable for their deeds.

Whoever believes in Allah and responds to His call will have eternal bliss in Paradise. And whoever turns away from the truth will be among the people of Hellfire.
## The Right of Allah upon Mankind is to Worship Him Alone and Not Associate Partners with Him

Worship is due only to the One who deserves it. Among the most important conditions for being worthy of worship is that the one being worshiped must be the Creator, the Sustainer, and the One possessing perfect attributes.

As for anything else—weak, helpless created beings who can neither benefit nor harm themselves—it is impermissible to devote any form of worship to them.

Worshiping idols and weak creatures is a deviation from sound human nature (fitrah), which affirms the obligation to worship Allah alone, the Creator of the universe.

Worship is a necessary duty upon us. It is the very purpose of our existence. Worship is the way we show gratitude and acknowledge the Lordship and Divinity of Allah. Whoever turns away from worshiping Allah or associates others with Him has wronged himself, deviated from the straight path intended by Allah, and will receive the consequences of his actions in the Hereafter.

Imagine someone gave you a million dollars—how would you react? Undoubtedly, you would thank him and praise him sincerely. Now suppose someone else offered you two million in exchange for your eyesight—would you accept? No rational person would.

Now reflect on this: it is Allah Who created you, honored you with hearing and sight, and surrounded you with countless blessings. He makes the blood flow through your veins, grants you water and air, and you need Him every moment. Without His mercy and care, you would perish. In fact, He provided for you while you were still a helpless fetus in your mother’s womb.

Doesn’t such a magnificent Creator deserve your gratitude and effort to seek His pleasure? Isn’t He worthy of being thanked for His blessings and believed in, along with the messengers He sent to guide you to truth and salvation?

Shouldn’t such a great Creator be worshiped the way *He* wants, not according to *your* desires? If you wanted to give your mother a gift, would you choose what pleases *you*, or would you strive to find what *she* loves in order to make her happy?

Likewise, worshiping Allah should not follow our inherited traditions or personal whims. It should be based on what Allah commands and approves.

Worship is not just rituals or customs—it is a declaration of total submission, sincere love for the Creator, and an acknowledgment that the One who created us and blessed us with His favors is the only One worthy of being worshiped without partners and in the manner He prescribed.

Shall we worship Allah as we see fit, when He alone is the All-Knowing, the All-Wise, who knows what truly benefits us and what pleases Him?

True worship springs from recognizing Allah’s greatness and feeling His favor and mercy. The more we obey His commands with sincerity, the closer we draw to Him and the more He is pleased with us.

Isn’t such a sacred relationship worth complete devotion, free from desires and blind traditions?
## Indeed, the Only Religion Acceptable to Allah is Islam

That is why Allah sent many prophets and messengers to inform us of the purpose of our existence and the goal of life. All of them came with one message: the message of Islam.

However, some people changed and distorted what the previous messengers brought, so Allah sent the final Messenger—Muhammad (peace be upon him)—and revealed to him the final message for humanity: the Qur’an.

There are many rational and scientific evidences proving that the Qur’an is truly the word of Allah and the only scripture that has remained preserved without alteration. Allah Himself informed us in the Qur’an that He will not accept any religion other than Islam, and that all messengers came with the same message—to believe in the One Creator and worship Him alone, without associating any partners.

What distinguishes Islam from all other religions is that Muslims are the only ones able to provide rational proofs and scientific facts—relying on trustworthy sources of knowledge—to prove that the Qur’an we have today is truly the preserved word of Allah, the Creator.

The Qur’an is the book Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Since more than 14 centuries ago, the Qur’an has challenged all of humanity to produce anything like it—even a single chapter—but they failed.

This challenge still stands today, and no one has ever been able to match the Qur’an in eloquence, clarity, divine wisdom, and legislation.

Allah says in the Qur’an:

**﴿قُل لَّئِنِ اجْتَمَعَتِ الإِنسُ وَالْجِنُّ عَلَىٰ أَن يَأْتُوا۟ بِمِثْلِ هَـٰذَا ٱلْقُرْءَانِ لَا يَأْتُونَ بِمِثْلِهِۦ وَلَوْ كَانَ بَعْضُهُمْ لِبَعْضٍۢ ظَهِيرًۭا﴾**
The meaning : “Say: If mankind and jinn were to come together to produce the like of this Qur’an, they could not produce the like thereof, even if they helped one another.”*
—Surah Al-Isra (17:88)

And yet, despite all human efforts, no one has ever produced anything like it. The miraculous nature of the Qur’an is not limited to its language alone—it also includes logical and scientific evidences that affirm it is from Allah.

The Qur’an speaks of scientific facts that were not discovered until centuries later, such as the stages of embryonic development in the womb. Allah says:

**﴿وَلَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ مِن سُلَـٰلَةٍۢ مِّن طِينٍۢ (١٢) ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَـٰهُ نُطْفَةًۭ فِى قَرَارٍۢ مَّكِينٍۢ (١٣) ثُمَّ خَلَقْنَا ٱلنُّطْفَةَ عَلَقَةًۭ فَخَلَقْنَا ٱلْعَلَقَةَ مُضْغَةًۭ فَخَلَقْنَا ٱلْمُضْغَةَ عِظَـٰمًۭا فَكَسَوْنَا ٱلْعِظَـٰمَ لَحْمًۭا ثُمَّ أَنشَأْنَـٰهُ خَلْقًا ءَاخَرَ فَتَبَارَكَ ٱللَّهُ أَحْسَنُ ٱلْخَـٰلِقِينَ﴾**
The meaning: *“And indeed We created man out of an extract of clay. Then We placed him as a drop of fluid in a firm resting place. Then We made the drop into a clinging clot, and We made the clot into a lump, and We made the lump bones, and We clothed the bones with flesh. Then We developed him into another creation. So blessed is Allah, the Best of creators.”*
—Surah Al-Mu’minun (23:12–14)

**The meaning:** These verses accurately describe the stages of human development, long before the invention of modern imaging and microscopes.

Who informed the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ about these scientific realities when no tools existed to observe them at the time?

This is just one of many examples that prove the Qur’an is of divine origin. The Qur’an is also the only book Allah guaranteed to preserve, as He said:

**﴿إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا ٱلذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَـٰفِظُونَ﴾**
The meaning: *“Indeed, it is We who sent down the Reminder [Qur’an], and indeed, We will be its Guardian.”*
—Surah Al-Hijr (15:9)

Throughout the centuries, the Qur’an has remained intact—memorized by millions, written precisely in copies, free of addition or omission—unlike other scriptures which were altered and changed.

The Qur’an brought comprehensive laws that organize individual and societal life, harmonizing between the soul and the body. No man-made philosophy has been able to achieve such balance.

Its laws speak to the intellect and align with human nature. It is not a man-made book, but the eternal message from Allah to all humanity—full of rational and scientific evidence, preserved from alteration, and a timeless proof of the truth of Islam.
## Why Islam?

A person might ask: Why Islam and not any other religion?

Allah sent messengers and revealed scriptures so that people may know who He is and how to worship Him. He informed us that this life is a test, and whoever worships Allah and does righteous deeds will be rewarded with Paradise. But those who turn away or worship others besides Him will be held accountable on the Day of Judgment.

Allah says:
**﴿إِنَّهُۥ مَن يَأْتِ رَبَّهُۥ مُجْرِمًۭا فَإِنَّ لَهُۥ جَهَنَّمَ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحْيَىٰ ۝ وَمَن يَأْتِهِۦ مُؤْمِنًۭا قَدْ عَمِلَ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ فَأُو۟لَـٰٓئِكَ لَهُمُ ٱلدَّرَجَـٰتُ ٱلْعُلَىٰ ۝ جَنَّـٰتُ عَدْنٍۢ تَجْرِى مِن تَحْتِهَا ٱلْأَنْهَـٰرُ خَـٰلِدِينَ فِيهَا ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَآءُ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ﴾**
The meaning: *“Indeed, whoever comes to his Lord as a criminal – for him is Hell; he will neither die therein nor live. But whoever comes to Him as a believer, having done righteous deeds – for them are the highest degrees – Gardens of perpetual residence beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide forever. And that is the reward of one who purifies himself.”*
—Surah Taha (20:74–76)

The religion brought by all prophets is Islam, which means submission and surrender to the will of the Creator, worshiping Him alone without partners, and believing in all His messengers.

The greatest honor that distinguishes human beings is the ability to reason. This ability enables them to reflect and search for truth.

Islam is a religion that aligns with sound reason and pure nature (fitrah). It provides logical answers to the major existential questions: Why are we here? What is the purpose of our life? What happens after death?

Islam requires belief in all the prophets. Muslims believe, as Allah tells us in the Qur’an, that Jesus (peace be upon him) was a servant and messenger of Allah—not the son of God. Allah is far above having a wife or a son. He is completely independent of such needs. Jesus was a noble prophet given miracles by Allah, and he called his people to worship Allah alone. Jesus never commanded people to worship him—rather, he himself worshiped his Lord.

If you want to be a true believer in Jesus, then you must embrace Islam and believe what Allah revealed in the Qur’an—the final, preserved message that confirms and renews the call to monotheism brought by all prophets before.

Allah tells us in the Qur’an that Jesus called his people to worship Allah alone and warned against associating others with Him:

**﴿لَّقَدْ كَفَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلْمَسِيحُ ٱبْنُ مَرْيَمَ ۚ وَقَالَ ٱلْمَسِيحُ يَـٰبَنِىٓ إِسْرَٰٓءِيلَ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّى وَرَبَّكُمْ ۖ إِنَّهُۥ مَن يُشْرِكْ بِٱللَّهِ فَقَدْ حَرَّمَ ٱللَّهُ عَلَيْهِ ٱلْجَنَّةَ وَمَأْوَىٰهُ ٱلنَّارُ ۖ وَمَا لِلظَّـٰلِمِينَ مِنْ أَنصَارٍۢ ۝ لَّقَدْ كَفَرَ ٱلَّذِينَ قَالُوٓا۟ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ ثَالِثُ ثَلَـٰثَةٍۢ ۘ وَمَا مِنْ إِلَـٰهٍ إِلَّآ إِلَـٰهٌۭ وَٰحِدٌۭ ۚ وَإِن لَّمْ يَنتَهُوا۟ عَمَّا يَقُولُونَ لَيَمَسَّنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ مِنْهُمْ عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٌ ﴾**
The meaning: *“They have certainly disbelieved who say, ‘Allah is the Messiah, the son of Mary’ while the Messiah has said, ‘O Children of Israel, worship Allah, my Lord and your Lord.’ Indeed, he who associates others with Allah – Allah has forbidden him Paradise, and his refuge is the Fire. And there are not for the wrongdoers any helpers. They have certainly disbelieved who say, ‘Allah is the third of three.’ And there is no god except one God. And if they do not desist from what they are saying, there will surely afflict the disbelievers among them a painful punishment.”*
—Surah Al-Ma’idah (5:72–73)
